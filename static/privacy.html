Bonwise privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Bonwise reads photos of shop receipts and shows where the same things are cheaper. This page explains what happens to your data.

Receipt photos and text

When you scan a receipt, the photo (or the text you paste) is sent over an encrypted connection to the Bonwise server. The server sends it to an AI model on Hugging Face Inference Providers (huggingface.co), run by Hugging Face or one of its hosting partners, which reads the lines and prices and sends them back. If the AI is unavailable, the server reads the photo itself with Tesseract OCR.

Bonwise does not store your photos or receipt contents on its server; they are processed and then discarded. Receipts can contain personal details such as a card’s last digits or a cashier’s name. Crop those out before scanning if you prefer.

Data on your phone

Your monthly budget, the receipts you save (shop, date, items and prices), your shopping list and your savings plan are saved on your device, in the app’s local storage. “Start fresh” in the app deletes them, and so does uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Household sharing (optional)

If you create or join a household, a copy of those receipts, the shopping list, the savings plan and the budget is stored on the Bonwise server so the phones in your household see the same data. It is linked only to the household code; the server stores a scrambled (hashed) form of the code, not the code itself, and keeps the data encrypted with a key made from the code, so the stored copy can’t be read without it. Anyone who has the code can see and change this data, so share it only with people you trust. “Delete household data” in Settings removes the copy from the server.

Community prices

When you add a receipt to your month, Bonwise sends the product names (in English), their prices, the shop chain (for example “LIDL”) and the date to the server, without any name, account, household code, device ID or IP address. These anonymous prices show all users where the same product was cheaper. You can switch this off in Settings.

Planning a shop

When you use “Going shopping?” or “Find cheapest shops for my list”, the items you typed are sent to the Bonwise server to look up prices. They are used only to answer that request and are not stored.

Your location

Only when you tap “Find shops near me”, “Find” (to compare shops for your list) or “Show the nearest shops” (for the swaps on a receipt) does the app ask for your location. If you have already allowed location for Bonwise, the nearest shops for a receipt’s swaps are shown straight away. It is rounded to about 100 metres. Your phone then asks a free OpenStreetMap map service for the shops in that area: Overpass (overpass-api.de), or when that is busy Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io, Germany). Like any website, that service sees your phone’s IP address. The shop list and the rounded position are sent to the Bonwise server to work out distances and prices. If your phone can’t reach them, the Bonwise server asks the same services instead. The server keeps shop lists it fetched itself in memory for up to six hours to answer repeat searches and never stores your location. If you don’t allow location, the app still shows the best prices it knows.

What the server keeps

  • Household data and anonymous community prices, as described above.
  • Your IP address is kept in memory for up to one hour to limit how many requests one device can make. It is not written to disk.
  • The server log records the time and the feature used (for example “POST /api/scan 200”), without photos, receipt contents, shopping items, locations or IP addresses.
  • To see whether Bonwise is useful, the server counts daily totals, for example “42 receipts scanned today” and which app language was used. These totals are not linked to any person, device, IP address or cookie, nothing is stored on your phone for them, and they are deleted after 400 days.
  • Your chosen app language is saved on your phone and sent with each request so the server can answer in that language. When you choose German, Turkish, Arabic or Hindi, the AI is asked to write its tips and product names in that language.

What Bonwise does not do

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking.
  • No selling of your data. Data is shared only as described above: receipts with the AI service to be read, and your rounded location with OpenStreetMap’s map service when you search for shops.

Other services

The app’s fonts are served by the Bonwise server itself, so no font service sees your IP address. The server is hosted by Render (render.com); all connections use HTTPS.

Children

Bonwise is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is stored about you and have it corrected or deleted. Because Bonwise has no accounts, most data is only on your phone; household data can be deleted in the app. For anything else, contact us below. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
