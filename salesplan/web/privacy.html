Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short

SalesPlan does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your article list, plans and settings, in the app's private storage on your device. With the app lock turned on, this data is encrypted (AES-256-GCM; the key is made from your PIN or password with PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds, and is never stored).

The Android app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.

Files you choose

When you import a file, the app reads it on the device. When you export a CSV file, a PDF or a backup, the app writes it only where you choose. Backup files are encrypted with a password you set. Where you send those files afterwards is up to you.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party code or services.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything deletes all data from the device. Uninstalling the app does the same. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so no copy is kept by Google.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the contact address on the app's Google Play page.
